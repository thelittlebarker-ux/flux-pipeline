Two products, one decision: where to charge, and when.

The fleet manager portal sets the rules and stays quiet until a van needs a person. The driver app turns a low battery into a single tap. Both are clickable prototypes with sample data for the Klang Valley rollout.

Driver app

  • Low-battery alert → three ranked stops by total stop time and cost
