# drivEV Growth Plan — Deepen B2B, Launch B2C, Win the Next 1,000

**By:** Yinson GreenTech · drivEV  ·  **Date:** 2026-06-16  ·  **Status:** Internal strategy
**Scope interpretation:** drivEV is B2B-led (fleet electrification, e.g. Pos Malaysia) and is now scaling B2C (retail lease/subscription + GoCar car-sharing). This plan keeps B2B as the cash-generative base and builds a repeatable B2C engine, with a concrete **"next 1,000 B2C drivers"** beachhead milestone on the path to 10,000+.

---

## 1 · Ambition & North-Star

**Mission:** make every electrified kilometre in Malaysia (then SG) cheaper, cleaner and more rewarding — so drivers and fleets stay, charge on chargEV, and refer others.

**North-Star Metric (NSM): Monthly Active, Retained Drivers (MARD)** — drivers (B2C subscribers + active fleet seats) who logged ≥8 trips and opened the app in the last 30 days. It links the three businesses: NSM ↑ → lease renewal ↑, chargEV sessions ↑, ESG impact ↑.

**12–18 month targets (illustrative):**
| Horizon | B2C drivers | Active fleet seats | NSM (MARD) | chargEV sessions/active driver/mo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Now | seed (GoCar/pilot) | existing fleets | baseline | baseline |
| **+2 quarters — "next 1,000"** | **1,000 B2C** | +2–3 new fleets | 70% of base | +20% |
| +4 quarters | 4,000 B2C | 6–8 fleets | 65%+ | +35% |
| +6 quarters | 10,000 B2C + SG pilot | 12+ fleets | 60%+ | +50% |

## 2 · Where we play (segments)

**B2B (deepen — the base):**
- **Last-mile / logistics fleets** (proven: Pos Malaysia 143+136 e-vans) — highest TCO pain, fastest payback.
- **Corporate & GLC fleets** (ESG mandates, NETR alignment) — sales/pool cars, executive fleets.
- **Ride-hail / taxi & shuttle operators** — high mileage = high EV savings.
- **Government & municipal** — policy tailwind.

**B2C (scale — the new S-curve):**
- **Urban professionals** in Klang Valley & Johor Bahru wanting an EV without 3-year lock-in or big upfront — drivEV's flexible-lease promise.
- **GoCar car-sharers** (existing Dec-2024 partnership in JB) — a warm, EV-curious funnel to convert to subscription.
- **B2B2C: fleet & corporate drivers** — give the drivEV app to fleet/employee drivers first; convert the best to personal B2C subscriptions ("drive it at work, lease it for home").

**Geography:** Malaysia first (Klang Valley + JB beachhead), Singapore as fast-follow (chargEV/CDG-ENGIE network + GoCar already cross-border).

## 3 · The growth flywheel (why the app is the engine)

```
 Flexible lease / car-share  →  drivEV app: telematics, savings, battery care
        ↑                                   ↓
 Referrals & word-of-mouth   ←  Eco-Credits & rewards (redeem on chargEV)
        ↑                                   ↓
 Lower CAC, higher LTV       ←  Stickiness → renewal + chargEV utilisation
                                            ↓
                         Better data → UBI/insurance discount → lower TCO → more demand
```

The app turns a commodity lease into a **daily, rewarding relationship**: it lowers churn (renewal), pulls demand into **chargEV** (a second revenue line), creates a **referral loop** (lower CAC), and produces **driving data** that unlocks **usage-based insurance discounts** (lower TCO → more conversions). Each loop compounds the others.

## 4 · Deepen B2B (the cash engine)

1. **Land-and-expand** within existing fleets: start with one depot/route, prove TCO + uptime via the app's fleet dashboard, then expand seats. Target **net seat retention >120%**.
2. **Fleet-Electrification-as-a-Service** bundle: vehicles + chargEV infrastructure + financing + driver app + carbon reporting (assurance-ready for clients' own ESG filings) — sold as one outcome, not parts.
3. **New verticals**: logistics → ride-hail → corporate → government, reusing the same playbook & case studies (Pos Malaysia as the anchor reference).
4. **B2B as a B2C funnel (B2B2C):** every fleet driver gets the app → seeds B2C habit, referrals, and warm leads for personal subscriptions.

## 5 · The "next 1,000" B2C playbook (the concrete milestone)

**Beachhead:** Klang Valley + Johor Bahru urban professionals + the existing **GoCar EV** user base. Win 1,000 paying B2C subscribers in ~2 quarters, then templatise.

**Acquisition channels (ranked by expected CAC efficiency):**
| Channel | Motion | Why it's efficient |
|---|---|---|
| **GoCar conversion** | Convert EV car-sharers → flexible subscription (in-app offer, trial credit) | Warm, EV-validated audience already in-funnel |
| **Referrals (in-app)** | Eco-Credits for referrer + referee; leaderboard kudos | Product-led, near-zero CAC; targets **K-factor ≥ 0.4** |
| **B2B2C / employer benefit** | "EV salary-sacrifice / staff lease" via existing fleet clients | Distribution through trusted employers; bulk |
| **chargEV cross-sell** | Promote drivEV to chargEV app users (366→3,000 charger footprint) | Owned audience, intent-rich |
| **Digital performance** | Savings-calculator landing page → lead → test drive | Measurable; calculator is strong TOFU |
| **Dealer/OEM & showroom** | Co-marketing with EV models drivEV leases (GAC, GWM, Maxus) | Buyer at decision point |

**Funnel & targets (illustrative):**
Awareness → **Savings Calculator** (the hook) → Lead → Test drive / GoCar trial → **Subscribe** → Activate (≥8 trips/mo) → Retain → Refer.
- Visitor→lead 6–8%, lead→subscriber 12–15%, activation ≥70%, 90-day retention ≥80%.
- **CAC target ≤ RM800 blended** (referrals/B2B2C pull it down); **LTV:CAC ≥ 3:1**; **CAC payback ≤ 6 months** on subscription margin + chargEV.

**Activation & retention (product-led):**
- Onboarding "connect & see your savings" in <60s; first-week challenge; streaks; weekly eco-score nudge; chargEV-credit reward at day 7 and day 30.
- Win-back + renewal offers surfaced in-app 60 days before lease end.

## 6 · Pricing & packaging

- **Flexible tiers:** short-commit subscription (premium price, max flexibility) → 12-month (best value) → fleet seat (volume). No mileage cap; transparent EIR financing (Consumer Credit Act-compliant).
- **Bundle chargEV:** included home-charging survey + monthly charging credit on higher tiers → ties the two businesses and improves stickiness.
- **UBI insurance discount** (score-and-refer via a licensed insurer) as a tier perk → lowers effective TCO, a conversion lever.
- **Referral economics** funded from CAC savings (give Eco-Credits, not cash) — keeps the rewards in the closed-loop e-money exemption.

## 11 · Risks & mitigations (incl. compliance)

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| B2C CAC too high / unproven | Lead with low-CAC channels (GoCar, referrals, B2B2C) before paid; hard LTV:CAC ≥ 3 gate |
| Charging anxiety blocks B2C | Bundle chargEV credit + home survey; in-app charger map/roaming |
| **PDPA at consumer scale** | Granular consent, DPO, DPIA, 72h breach runbook (see compliance research) before scaling |
| **Eco-Credits = regulated e-money** | Keep closed-loop, earned-not-purchased (limited-purpose exemption); no cash-out |
| **B2C financing licensing** | Align lease/financing to Consumer Credit Act 2025 / CCC + HP Amendment (EIR) |
| **Insurance-reward licensing** | Score-and-refer via licensed insurer; legal sign-off (FSA 2013) |
| Greenwashing on savings claims | Substantiate CO₂ (ISO 14064-1/GHG Protocol); assurance-ready for NSRF |
| EV incentive changes (road-tax exemption ended 2025) | Compete on flexibility + TCO + rewards, not just incentives |
