Market entry08 / 12

We will fight a price war — the question is who funds the discount

The first two months need acquisition firepower against Flux. Before we launch, the Board must decide the funding and cap the burn.

The decision

Does drivEV or the host partner fund the introductory discount? This single choice changes our cash exposure.

The discipline

Size the war chest to a defined CAC and share-of-voice target, with a hard stop. We don't win on price alone — chargEV + the B2B bridge lower effective CAC.
