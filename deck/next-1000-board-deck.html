The reframe02 / 11

The next 1,000 is a portfolio, not a funnel.

The growth plan headlines “1,000 B2C drivers.” The economics say: win the 1,000 net-new units across four channels where distribution already exists — and let the B2B base fund the B2C S-curve.

  • ~75%comes through relationships we already own — B2B land-and-expand plus the salary-sacrifice bridge. Only ~25% is cold-acquired B2C.
  • ↓ CACThese channels aren’t CAC-constrained. Acquisition is a sales touch inside a payroll or fleet account, not paid media.
  • RMThey’re capital- and residual-value-constrained. So this deck is about the balance sheet, not the funnel.

The risk that actually moves returns08 / 11

Residual value, not churn, is the swing factor.

At M36 the book carries ~RM109m of expected residual value. A −15% used-EV price shock erases ~RM16.4m — about two-thirds of peak equity. This is the number that decides the equity return.

→
Mitigations (not in the cashflows)
Push RV guarantees / buyback onto OEMs & distributors at order time · favour shorter tenor on price-volatile models · diversify make/model to avoid single-platform RV risk.
